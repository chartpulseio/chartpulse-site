How to read it. Positive gamma (price above the flip): dealers hedge against the move, so ranges hold and dips get bought. Negative gamma (below the flip): dealers hedge with the move, so breaks extend and volatility expands. The call wall is the strike with the most positive call gamma (often resistance); the put wall is the most negative put gamma (often support). Vanna shows how dealer deltas shift if implied volatility moves; charm shows the delta drift as time passes, strongest into expiry.
Method. Exposure = gamma × open interest × contract size × spot² × 1%, calls positive and puts negative (the standard assumption that customers buy calls and puts and dealers take the other side). It is a model of positioning, not a certainty. Open interest updates once a day; US option quotes use Alpaca's indicative feed. Information only, not investment advice.
