Strength is a checklist score, not a probability. It is the stage base (Watch 20, Early 35, Confirmed 50, Best 65) plus 15 for each of the three filters that agrees — EMA slope, volume on the trigger bar, and the weekly trend — capped at 95. The thresholds (1.5 ATR approach zone, 20-bar sweep, 3-bar trigger window, 1.2× volume) are starting values and have not been backtested on this universe. Early and Watch will produce more false signals than Confirmed and Best. Watch rows are a watchlist, not entries. While a market is open the current daily candle is included, so Early and Confirmed rows can appear and disappear before the close; they are final after it.
