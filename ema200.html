200 EMA Scanner

Daily 200 EMA entries, from the earliest read to the safest. A plain cross is noisy, so each symbol is placed in one of four stages instead: price approaching the average, a first trigger near it, a confirmed close through it, and a retest that held. The four stages are read on the daily chart. Each row also says which timeframe (1D, 4H, 1H, 30m, 15m) has price at its own 200 EMA right now: "in the ATR zone" means price is within one ATR of that chart's 200 EMA, and "closed above / below" means a candle on that chart closed across it in the last five candles.

How rows are ranked. Best setups come first. A Top setup has the bigger trend with it (MTF trend on 30m, 1H, 4H, 1D and 1W, at least 4 of 5 agreeing), a Confirmed or Best stage, a key zone sitting on the 200 EMA (today's high or low, previous day high, low or close, weekly or monthly high or low, a pivot or a Fibonacci level) and at least two of the three filters. A Good setup has the trend with it plus either the stage or the zone. Everything else is a Basic setup. Take only the side shown: CALL means look to buy, PUT means look to sell. The grade is a rule-based checklist, not a win probability; it has not been backtested on this universe. Early and Watch will produce more false signals than Confirmed and Best. Watch rows are a watchlist, not entries. While a market is open the current daily candle is included, so rows can appear and disappear before the close.
