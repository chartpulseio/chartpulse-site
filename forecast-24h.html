24-Hour AI Forecast

The likely price path and price range for the next 24 hours on forex, crypto and gold, from three AI forecast models (Chronos-2, TimesFM 2.5, Moirai 2.0). Refreshed every hour from closed 1-hour candles, and checked one day later.

Use the range, not the direction. These models are good at saying how far price may travel (the shaded band) and close to a coin toss on which way. For learning and planning stops and targets, not for bets.

How to use it

Stops and targets
A stop inside the band is likely to be touched by normal noise. A target far outside the band is unlikely to be reached in a day.
Leaning up / down
Where the middle line ends after 24 hours compared with the price now. "3 of 3" means all models lean the same way. It is a lean, not a signal.
Market closed
On weekends forex does not trade. The forecast then starts from Friday's last candle and applies to the first hours after the open.

Not financial advice. Forecasts are model output from past prices only. News can move price far outside any band.
