    How it works
    
  
    
    
    STEP 1You write, in plain words
    When to buy, when to sell, and your stop-loss. English works best; simple Hinglish is fine too.
    
    
    STEP 2It shows what it understood
    Your words become exact rules, listed back to you, so you can check them before trusting any number.
    
    
    STEP 3It tests on real history
    Every trade is simulated with costs, and compared with simply buying and holding.

    For education and demonstrations, not financial advice. A backtest shows what would have happened in the past on one market; it does not predict the future. Trades are filled on the next bar with 0.1% commission and 0.05% slippage per side. Chart patterns, news, order flow and intraday session rules cannot be tested here. Always read "What I understood" first: if a rule is not what you meant, rewrite that sentence more simply.
