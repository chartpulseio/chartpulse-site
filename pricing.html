How does the free trial work?

The trial is measured in live Tier-1 signals, not calendar days, so a slow week or a market holiday never uses it up. Leave your details and we'll switch it on for you.

Why are prices in India lower?

Visitors in India see regional pricing in rupees: the global price converted to INR, then reduced by 25%. Everyone else pays in US dollars. You can switch currency at the top of the page.

How do the longer billing periods work?

You pay once for the whole period: quarterly saves 10%, half-yearly 20% and yearly 30% against paying monthly. There is no auto-debit: nothing renews by itself, and you renew only when you choose to.

What is the TradingView Setup add-on?

The Tier-1 view and today's Pulse levels on your own TradingView charts, with help to set it up. Add it to Starter or Core.

Tier 2 is the execution view that comes with Core. Open it from the TIER 2 link on the Tier-1 scanner: a 10-minute chart with the action line, target and stop-loss, a 12-factor confluence engine, the symbol's news and one-tap Telegram alerts.
