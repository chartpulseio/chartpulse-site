ChartPulse God-father Scanner

All ChartPulse scanners work together here. A setup gets an A+ only when the big trend, a Power Zone (trend lines from several charts, today's high or low, key levels, the 200 EMA, order blocks), an entry trigger and the news check all line up. Everything else waits.

  1. 1 · TRENDBig trend decides

    Daily trend, with the 4H or Weekly agreeing. Up = CALL only. Down = PUT only.

  2. 2 · ZONEPower Zone

    Trend lines with 2+ touches on any chart, Day High/Low, PDH/PDL, Fib, pivot, 200 EMA, OB/FVG. More levels in one place = stronger.

  3. 3 · SETUPTrend line trade

    Rejection at the line, a fake breakout (trap), a breakout + retest, or a Day High/Low reversal.

  4. 4 · TRIGGERThe turn prints

    A rejection or failed-break candle, a Tier-1 tick, a 200 EMA entry or a Day Drive signal.

  5. 5 · CHECKNews & volume

    High-impact news against the trade blocks it. Volume, momentum and news with it lift the grade.

How to read a card

A+
Big trend, a Power Zone of 4+ levels with a trend line AND today's high or low in it, a strong line (4H/Daily/Weekly or 3+ touches), a real turn candle at the line or a Tier-1 tick, a real-time price feed (markets whose feed runs 15-30 min late are capped at A), and a second scanner family agreeing on Daily, 1H or 15m (EMA momentum, volume/VWAP or news), with no Daily momentum scanner against it. The best the board has.
A
The turn has printed at a double zone (2 levels). A good trade.
B
The turn has printed, but only at a single level. Smaller size, or skip.
Get ready
Price is at a double or Power Zone in the trend's direction, but no turn candle yet. Watch it closely.
Watch
Close to a zone, or the market is closed right now.
News risk
A fresh high-impact story points against the trade. Wait until it settles.

Fake breakout (trap)
Price closed through the line and then closed back. Breakout traders are trapped and the move often runs hard the other way.
Breakout + retest
A line against the trend broke, and price came back to it. The old line now holds.
