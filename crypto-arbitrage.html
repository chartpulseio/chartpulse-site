Crypto Arbitrage Scanner

Two kinds of crypto arbitrage on one page, free: funding rate arbitrage (get paid funding while price moves cancel out) and exchange price gaps (the same coin cheaper on one exchange than another). Read from seven exchanges, refreshed every minute.

  1. 1 · FUNDING CARRYSpot + perpetual

    Buy the coin, short the same amount on its perpetual. While funding is positive, the short is paid.

  2. 2 · FUNDING SPREADExchange vs exchange

    Short the perpetual where funding is high, long it where funding is low. You keep the difference.

  3. 3 · PRICE GAPBuy here, sell there

    Cheapest ask on one exchange against the highest bid on another, after trading fees.

Before you trade any of these

Funding changes
The rate shown is the current one. It is reset every 1, 4 or 8 hours and can turn negative. The yearly figure only says what you would earn if it stayed the same.
Fees first
"Days to cover fees" is how long funding must stay at this level just to pay for opening and closing both sides.
Price gaps are often closed doors
A big gap usually means deposits or withdrawals are paused on one exchange, or the two tickers are different coins. This page cannot see that. Check both exchanges before sending money.
Speed and size
Small gaps disappear in seconds and the best price only holds for a small amount.
How is this different from the Squeeze scanner?
The Liquidation Squeeze Scanner uses funding to find crowded traders at a chart level and gives a CALL or PUT. This page gives no direction: both sides are hedged and you look at the yield or the gap.
Margin risk
A short perpetual can be liquidated in a sharp rally even when the spot coin covers it. Keep spare margin.
