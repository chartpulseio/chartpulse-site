How this engine fills a trade

No lookahead. A rule is tested on a closed bar; the order fills at the open of the bar after it. A higher-timeframe indicator only ever uses closed higher-timeframe candles.
The bad case wins. If a stop and a target are both touched inside one bar, the stop is taken. On a gap the fill is the open price, not your level.
Costs are real. Commission applies on both sides; slippage applies on market fills and stops, never on a limit target. Position size follows equity, so compounding is included.
Ruin is final. If equity reaches zero the position is force-closed and trading stops, exactly as a broker would.
