Hybrid Trend Line Scanner

Trades with the bigger trend only. In a downtrend it looks for a PUT where today's high meets a resistance line. In an uptrend it looks for a CALL where today's low meets a support line. It alerts when price is rejected at the line, or breaks through it and comes back.

  1. STEP 1Trend decides the side

    The Daily trend is the veto. The 4-hour or the Weekly trend must agree with it. Down means PUT only, up means CALL only.

  2. STEP 2Day high or low at a line

    PUT: today's high is at a resistance trend line. CALL: today's low is at a support trend line.

  3. STEP 3Signal, then the turn

    Price at the line is the signal. It becomes an alert when a candle touches the line and closes away, or closes through it and the next closes back.

  4. LABELSZones and pullback

    A key zone on the line, and 30m / 1H running against the trend into the line, are marked on each setup.

Words used on this page

Failed break
A candle closed through the line, then a candle closed back on the other side. The breakout did not hold. This is the strongest alert.
Rejection
A candle reached the line and closed away from it. The line held.
At the line
Price is on the line, a little under or a little over it, with the trend against it. This is the signal: the place to look for the trade.
Broken, waiting
Price is clearly through the line right now. No trade yet: the scanner is waiting to see whether it comes back.
Approaching
Price is close to the line and the day's high or low is already at it. Get ready, nothing to do yet.
Pullback
The 30-minute or 1-hour trend is running against the bigger trend, into the line. That is the move this scanner wants to fade.
Major / strong zone
The line also sits on a previous day high, low or close, a weekly or monthly high or low, a pivot or a Fibonacci level. One match is a major zone, two different kinds a strong zone.

An alert counts as live only while that market is trading; turns from a closed market are listed lower as "Last session". Lines from the 15-minute and 30-minute charts need three touches. Trend is the standard Supertrend (10, 3). Trend lines come from the Daily, 4-hour, 1-hour, 30-minute and 15-minute charts. The turn is read on 15-minute candles where intraday data is available, otherwise on 1-hour candles. Stop and targets on a signal or alert are worked out by rule; no win rate is attached because these rules have not been measured. Education and demonstration only, not financial advice.
