Next Candle AI

Four AI forecast models (Kronos, Chronos-2, TimesFM 2.5, Moirai 2.0) each guess whether the next 5-minute candle closes up or down. When all four agree on a forex pair, it shows here as a call. Every call is graded against the real candle, and the real hit rate is below.

For fun and learning. Not for bets. In our test this rule was right about 53 to 58 times in 100, only a little better than a coin toss, and one model alone was a coin toss. Do not put money on it.

  1. 1 · CLOSED CANDLESNo peeking

    The models only see candles that have already closed. The guess is for the candle that is forming now.

  2. 2 · FOUR MODELSAll must agree

    A call needs 4 of 4 the same way, on a forex pair. Anything else is shown as "no call".

  3. 3 · GRADEDRight or wrong

    Five minutes later the real close decides. Wins and losses are counted as they are.

How to read this

What is a call?
All four models expect the next 5-minute close to be on the same side of the last close, on a forex pair.
What is "Strong"?
The models also expect a bigger move than usual (top fifth of recent calls).
What is the next-hour range?
The price band where the models expect the price to be one hour from now, about 8 times out of 10. It says how far price may travel, not which way.
Why forex only?
That is the only market where the rule held up in both halves of our test. Crypto and gold are shown for interest, without calls.
How good is it really?
In our walk-forward test on 5-minute candles, all four agreeing on forex was right 53% to 58% of the time.
When is it quiet?
Forex is closed from Friday night to Sunday night (UTC). The page shows calls again when candles start printing.

Not financial advice and not a betting tool. Short-term price direction is close to random; these models are shown so you can watch how AI forecasts behave on live markets.
