News Scanner

Which markets are in the news right now, and what the news is saying. One row per market: the headlines, their tone, how far price has moved since, and whether the news agrees with the trend.

  1. STEP 1In the news

    A market is listed when at least one story in the last 48 hours names it.

  2. STEP 2Tone of the headlines

    Each story is read as positive, negative or neutral. More positive than negative = "News points UP", and the reverse.

  3. STEP 3Did price react?

    The move since the first story shows whether price has already answered the news.

  4. STEP 4News + trend

    The label says whether the news points the same way as the bigger trend. That is the cleaner case.

Reading the news…

Words used on this page

News points UP / DOWN
Most of the stories about this market read positive (or negative). It describes the headlines, it is not a forecast.
Mixed
Positive and negative stories together, or only neutral ones. No clear direction from the news.
High impact
The story is about something that usually moves markets: interest rates, inflation, war, tariffs, a crash, a default.
Since the news
How far price has moved since just before the first of these stories was published.
News + trend agree
The headlines point the same way as the trend (Daily, confirmed by the 4-hour or the Weekly).
Already moved
Price has gone 0.3% or more in the direction of the news. The easy part of the move may be over.

For education and demonstrations, not financial advice. Tone is read from the words in each headline and summary; it can be wrong, so open the story before acting. Stories come from public news feeds and are matched to markets by name. Trend is the standard Supertrend (10, 3). The page refreshes every minute; the news feed itself every 10 minutes.
