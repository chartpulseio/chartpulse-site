How Tier-1 works

One rulebook, applied the same way to every instrument, running on our servers.

Open the scanner
01

Pulse Sync

The engine reads four trend layers, from fast to slow, for every instrument. Only a clear, synced trend enters the scanner; a split never does.

Trend layers
02

Pulse zone

Each day has a Pulse Floor and a Pulse Ceiling. CALL fires only at the Floor and PUT only at the Ceiling. The remark tells you whether it is a long or a small target. Until then the symbol is Under process.
