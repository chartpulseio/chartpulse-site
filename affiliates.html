Program rules

Which purchases count?

Starter and Core plans, on any billing period. Courses and the TradingView setup add-on do not carry a discount or a commission.

How is the commission worked out?

20% of the amount the customer actually paid. Payments in rupees are converted to US dollars at the reference rate shown on the pricing page.

Why is there a 30-day hold?

So that a refunded or reversed payment never becomes a payout. A refunded sale earns no commission.

When and how am I paid?

Once a month, in USDT, to the address you save in your dashboard, when your payable balance is $50 or more. Smaller balances carry over to the next month.

Which customer belongs to me?

The first code a buyer uses stays with that buyer. Their renewals count for you even if they do not type the code again.

What is not allowed?

Using your own code on your own purchase, fake sign-ups, spam, paid ads on the ChartPulse brand name, and promising profits. ChartPulse is for education and demonstrations; do not present it as financial advice. Accounts that break these rules can be closed and unpaid commission cancelled.
