    How to read order flow

    Delta and CVD
    Delta is market buys minus market sells in one candle. CVD adds them up. Price up with CVD up is a healthy move; price up while CVD falls means the rise is not backed by buyers.
    Futures vs spot CVD
    Spot buying is real coins changing hands; futures can be leverage. Moves led by spot tend to last longer.
    Price and open interest together
    Up + OI up: new longs. Up + OI down: short covering. Down + OI up: new shorts. Down + OI down: longs closing or liquidated.
    Funding
    Positive funding means longs pay shorts: the crowd leans long. Very high funding often comes before a flush.
    Liquidation heatmap
    Price is often pulled toward large clusters, because forced orders there add fuel. It is an estimate made from open interest, as every liquidation heatmap is.
    Order book walls
    Big resting orders can slow price, but they can be pulled at any time. Treat them as hints, not floors.

    Sources.
