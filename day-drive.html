What backs each requirement

The model asks for footprint and order-book reads. This page runs on ordinary bar data, so every requirement is listed with what actually stands behind it — and the one thing that cannot be substituted is marked unavailable rather than guessed.

The score is a checklist total, not a probability. It adds up which conditions are met, with weights set by hand. A score of 90 does not mean a 90% chance of anything, and this model has not yet been measured on this universe — until it has, treat the score as "how much of the checklist is ticked" and nothing more.
Not advice. This page shows what a rule set flags, not a recommendation, and nothing here is a promise about the future. Flow figures are estimates from bar shape, not order-flow data. Trade your own plan and your own risk.
