Words used on this page

CALL / PUT
CALL means the scanner is looking for price to rise. PUT means it is looking for price to fall.
Score
A checklist total out of 100: check 1 is worth 35, check 2 is 25, check 3 is 40. It is not a win probability and has not been measured as one.
Stop-loss
The price where the idea is wrong and the trade should be closed.
VWAP
Today's average traded price. Above it buyers are ahead, below it sellers are.
Liquidity sweep
Price runs just past an obvious level, triggers the stops there, then turns back.
Change of character (CHoCH)
The first break of a swing against the recent move, an early sign of a turn.

Some inputs are estimates and are marked "est." on the detail page: buying and selling pressure and one-sided candles are read from where each candle closes, because order-book data is not available here. This page is for education and demonstration. It is not financial advice.
