Trend Line Scanner

Every market is checked on seven charts, from weekly down to 10-minute. The scanner draws the trend line each chart is respecting, finds where price has just broken a line or is testing one, and labels every CALL and PUT signal with its trend, zone and line strength. Tap a row for the charts, entry, stop and targets.

  1. STEP 1Find the line

    Swing highs make a resistance line, swing lows a support line. A line dies when a candle closes through it.

  2. STEP 2Spot the event

    A candle closed through the line (a break), or price is sitting on the line now (a test). Every one is listed.

  3. STEP 3Label it

    MTF trend (Supertrend 10, 3 on 30m, 1H, 4H, 1D, 1W), key zones, touches and pattern are added as labels. Nothing is filtered out.

  4. THENBest setups first

    Each row shows its quality factors as labels: trend, zone, breakout or test, touches, chart and pattern. More green ticks rank higher.

New to this? Use it in four steps

  1. Every signal is listed, best first. Read the labels: green ticks are in its favour, a red label is a warning. Top setups only narrows the list if you want fewer.
  2. CALL means look to buy, PUT means look to sell. The MTF trend label tells you whether the bigger trend is with that side or against it.
  3. Open the row. The trade plan gives the entry, the stop-loss and two targets. Do not enter before the entry condition happens.
  4. Risk a small, fixed part of your account on each trade, and always place the stop-loss.

Words used on this page

Resistance line
A line across swing highs. Price has turned down from it before; a close above it is a breakout.
Support line
A line across swing lows. Price has bounced from it before; a close below it is a breakdown.
Touches
How many separate times price reached the line and respected it. More touches make a stronger line; the count is shown on every signal.
Channel
Two lines sloping the same way. Falling channel: sellers in control. Rising channel: buyers in control.
Triangle and wedge
Two lines closing in on each other. Price is being squeezed and usually leaves with a strong move.
Major / strong zone
The line sits on a key level: today's high or low, previous day high, low or close, weekly or monthly high or low, a pivot or a Fibonacci level. One match is a major zone, two or more a strong zone.
MTF bias
Trend direction on 30m, 1H, 4H, 1D and 1W. Standard Supertrend (10, 3). CALL bias when at least 4 of 5 point up, PUT bias when at least 4 of 5 point down (3 of 4 where there is no 30m data). It is a label on each signal, not a filter.

Lines are drawn by rule from swing highs and lows on the last 140 candles of each chart. They describe the chart; they are not a forecast. The grade (Top, Good, Basic) comes from the labelled factors on each row. It is a rule-based checklist, not a win probability; it has not been measured. Intraday charts are shown for markets with recent intraday data. Education and demonstration only, not financial advice.
