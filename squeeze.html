Liquidation Squeeze Scanner

When too many traders pile into one side with leverage, a small move against them forces them out, and the forced exits push price further. This scanner finds coins where longs or shorts are crowded (funding rate and open interest) at a key zone, and flags the ones where the squeeze has started.

  1. 1 · CROWDEDOne side pays

    High positive funding: longs crowded (PUT side). Negative funding: shorts crowded (CALL side).

  2. 2 · BUILDINGOpen interest rising

    More leveraged positions keep opening on the crowded side.

  3. 3 · READYAt a key zone

    Crowded longs at today's high or a resistance line; crowded shorts at today's low or a support line. Zones are read for the 98 coins ChartPulse tracks; other liquid coins show crowding only.

  4. 4 · SQUEEZE ONThe flush starts

    Price moves against the crowd while open interest drops: positions are being closed or liquidated.
