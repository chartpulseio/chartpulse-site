      Trendline Hero

      One simple idea: trade with the big trend, at a trend line, where today's high or low is. Trend down: look for a PUT at a resistance line that is also today's high. Trend up: look for a CALL at a support line that is also today's low. Lines from big charts (4-hour, Daily, Weekly) get longer targets.

      1. STEP 1Find the big trend

        The Daily trend decides. The 4-hour or the Weekly trend must agree with it. Trend down = PUT only. Trend up = CALL only.

      2. STEP 2Line + today's high or low

        PUT: today's high sits on a resistance trend line. CALL: today's low sits on a support trend line. Lines come from 30m, 1H, 4H, Daily and Weekly charts.

      3. STEP 3Wait for the turn

        Price at the line is the signal. It becomes an alert when a 30-minute candle touches the line and closes away, or closes through it and then closes back.

      4. STEP 4Entry, stop, targets

        Each signal shows the entry, the stop-loss and two targets. A bigger chart's line gets a longer second target. Zones on the line are labelled.

      Words used on this page

      Failed break
      A candle closed through the line, then a candle closed back on the other side. The breakout did not hold. This is the strongest alert.
      Rejection
      A candle reached the line and closed away from it. The line held.
      At the line
      Price is on the line, a little under or a little over it, with the trend against it. This is the signal: the place to look for the trade.
      Broken, waiting
      Price is clearly through the line right now. No trade yet: the scanner is waiting to see whether it comes back.
      Approaching
      Price is close to the line and the day's high or low is already at it. Get ready, nothing to do yet.
      Pullback
      The 30-minute or 1-hour trend is running against the bigger trend, into the line. That is the move this scanner wants to fade.
      Major / strong zone
      The line also sits on a previous day high, low or close, a weekly or monthly high or low, a pivot or a Fibonacci level. One match is a major zone, two different kinds a strong zone.

      For education and demonstrations, not financial advice. An alert counts as live only while that market is trading; turns from a closed market are listed lower as "Last session". Trend is the standard Supertrend (10, 3). Trend lines come from the Weekly, Daily, 4-hour, 1-hour and 30-minute charts; a 30-minute line needs three touches. Targets: 30m and 1H lines 1.5 and 2.5 times the risk; a 4H line 2 and 3.5 times; a Daily or Weekly line 2 and 5 times, or the other line of the pattern when that is further.
